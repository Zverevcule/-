Common questions

How is the price set?

Once you describe the problem and send a photo, the San3a team reviews it with the technician and sends you a labour price range before any visit. Materials are priced separately and agreed before work starts. Nothing begins without your approval.

What if the price changes after the inspection?

If the inspection shows the problem is bigger than described, the technician stops until we come back to you with the new price and you approve it. If you don’t agree, you can stop there without committing to any extra work.

What does the guarantee cover?

A 15-day guarantee on workmanship from the day of the job. If the same problem returns within that time, we send the technician back to fix it at no extra labour cost. Materials and parts carry the manufacturer’s or seller’s warranty.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re starting in Hurghada: the main residential districts and nearby resort areas. If your area isn’t listed, choose “Other area” and type it in. We’ll tell you if we can reach it.
