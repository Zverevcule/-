How it works

San3a is the accountable link between you and the technician: we coordinate, follow up and take responsibility if something goes wrong.

Four steps, followed up at every one

  1. 01

    Request and describe

    Choose the service, describe the fault, add a photo and your address.

  2. 02

    We match a technician

    We pick a vetted technician available in your area and confirm the time.

  3. 03

    Approve the price first

    You get a price range based on your description and photos. No work starts without your approval.

  4. 04

    Done, checked, guaranteed

    The technician does the job, we follow up until it’s finished, and it’s covered for 15 days.

The price is set after you describe the problem and send a photo

We don’t publish a fixed price list, because the same service can vary a lot depending on the fault. Instead:

  • A labour price range before the visit, based on your description, photos and local Hurghada rates.
  • Materials are separate from labour; you see and approve them before work starts.
  • No extras without your approval. If the inspection reveals more, the technician pauses and we check with you.
  • You pay after the job, once you’ve checked it’s done.

A clear way to complain, and someone accountable

The biggest worry with repairs is that nobody answers once the technician leaves. At San3a, your request has a number and someone tracking it.

  • Every request has a number you can quote.
  • We ask for your feedback after the job, and your rating affects who we recommend next time.
  • Every complaint is reviewed and we propose a fix: a return visit, a correction or fair compensation.

Everything you need to know

How is the price set?

Once you describe the problem and send a photo, the San3a team reviews it with the technician and sends you a labour price range before any visit. Materials are priced separately and agreed before work starts. Nothing begins without your approval.

What if the price changes after the inspection?

If the inspection shows the problem is bigger than described, the technician stops until we come back to you with the new price and you approve it. If you don’t agree, you can stop there without committing to any extra work.

What does the guarantee cover?

A 15-day guarantee on workmanship from the day of the job. If the same problem returns within that time, we send the technician back to fix it at no extra labour cost. Materials and parts carry the manufacturer’s or seller’s warranty.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re starting in Hurghada: the main residential districts and nearby resort areas. If your area isn’t listed, choose “Other area” and type it in. We’ll tell you if we can reach it.

What if there’s a problem with the technician?

San3a is responsible for your request. Contact us with your request number and we’ll handle the complaint ourselves: a return visit, a correction or another fair solution.

How do I pay?

For now you pay after the job is done and you’ve checked it, in cash or by transfer where available. More payment options will come with the app.
